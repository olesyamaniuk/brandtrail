Gameplay and Controls

The gameplay is straightforward: tap to make your whale flap its fins and fly upwards, release to let it glide down. Navigate through floating islands, avoid obstacles, and try to keep your whale airborne for as long as possible. 
Collect bubbles scattered across the sky to increase your score and unlock new trails that change the look of your whale’s path. The challenge grows gradually, keeping the experience fresh without ever overwhelming you.
